# Issue plan: The Independent, "What changed my mind" (busy vs. productive)

I don't know what you said last year, what you got wrong, or what you think now, so I haven't made those up. Wherever the plan needs one of them, there's a bracketed placeholder for you to fill in. The structure, hooks and subject lines are built to work once those are filled.

## Sharpened topic

**Started with:** "The difference between being busy and being productive", revisited as "here's what I said last year, here's what I got wrong, here's what I think now."

**Arrived at:** "Last year I told solo consultants that [your position last year]. I was wrong about [the specific part you got wrong], and I now think [your current view], because [what changed your mind]."

Once the brackets are filled, this is a claim a reader can agree or disagree with. To keep it arguable, make [your current view] a position a freelancer could push back on, not a softer version of last year's view.

## Hook/headline options

- **Direct:** "I was wrong about busy vs. productive. Here's what I believe now: [your current view in one line]."
- **Curiosity:** "A year ago I gave you advice on being productive instead of busy. One part of it didn't hold up."
- **Contrarian:** "'[your position last year, quoted or paraphrased]' sounds right to most freelancers. I said it too. Here's why I've stopped believing it."
