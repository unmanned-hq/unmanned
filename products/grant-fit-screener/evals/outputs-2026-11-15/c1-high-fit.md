# Grant Fit Memo: Great Lakes Environmental Fund

- "reduce agricultural runoff"
- "501(c)(3) nonprofits operating in the Great Lakes watershed"
- "at least 3 years of environmental restoration experience"
- "a minimum annual budget of $200,000"

## Verdict

**High.** Eligibility and capacity are Strong and mission is Partial, so this is worth a full proposal as long as the project clearly addresses agricultural runoff.

## Next steps: watch-out list

These are the three RFP requirements most likely to come out thin in a first draft:

1. **"Reduce agricultural runoff."** Your profile talks about wetlands and stream corridors, not farms. A first draft may present a strong restoration project without showing how it cuts runoff from agricultural land. Name the agricultural land involved and explain how the work reduces what runs off it.
2. **"Restore riparian buffers."** Your portfolio also includes wetland work, so the draft could drift toward wetland restoration. Keep the project, the deliverables and your track record focused on riparian buffers in particular.
3. **"Operating in the Great Lakes watershed."** You are based in Michigan and work in the lower watershed, but the proposal has to show that this specific project site is inside the Great Lakes watershed. Don't let reviewers assume it.
