# Grant Fit Memo: Federal Rural Health Care Access Program (NOFO #HHS-2026-HRSA-RH-0042)

## Funder priorities (their words)

- "Federal Rural Health Care Access Program"
- "Federally Qualified Health Centers (FQHCs), FQHC Look-Alikes, and Rural Health Clinics (RHCs)"
- "in Health Professional Shortage Areas (HPSAs) designated as rural under OMB standards"
- "must not have received an HRSA Rural Health grant in the prior two award cycles"
- "$150,000–$500,000 for 3-year projects" with a "10% non-federal cash match"

## Dimension scores

| Dimension | Score | One-line reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mission alignment | Partial | You serve uninsured and underinsured patients, but your profile does not say you serve a rural population, and rural access is the program's focus. |
| Eligibility | Weak | None of the hard criteria is confirmed: Look-Alike status is a belief, HPSA designation is unconfirmed, rural status under OMB standards is not stated, and the prior-funding restriction is unresolved. |
| Capacity | Partial | You have one prior HRSA award (FY2022), but its size and scope, your annual budget, your staffing and your ability to put up a 10% cash match are not stated. |

## Verdict

**Low.** You cannot show that you are eligible yet, and until you can, a full proposal risks being screened out before anyone reads it.

## Blockers

1. **Organisation type is unconfirmed.** You "believe" you are an FQHC Look-Alike. The NOFO accepts only FQHCs, FQHC Look-Alikes and RHCs. You need documented status, not a belief.
2. **HPSA designation is unconfirmed.** You have not confirmed HPSA designation for your current service area. This is a hard requirement.
3. **Rural designation under OMB standards: Not stated.** The NOFO requires the HPSA to be "designated as rural under OMB standards." Nothing in your profile confirms this for your service area, so treat it as unmet until you verify it.
4. **Prior-funding restriction is unresolved.** You received HRSA funding in FY2022. Two things are not stated: whether that award was an *HRSA Rural Health grant*, and how long this program's award cycles are. If it was a Rural Health grant and FY2022 falls within the prior two cycles, you are ineligible.
5. **Match capacity: Not stated.** A 10% non-federal cash match is required. At the top award of $500,000, that is $50,000 in cash from non-federal sources. You have not said whether you can raise it.

Clear blockers 1 through 4 with documentation before you spend any writing hours. If all four check out, send your annual budget and staffing and run this screen again. The verdict could then move up.
