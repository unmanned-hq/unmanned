**Where Reviewer 2's comment contradicts itself**

1. **Verdict vs. uncertainty.** The reviewer says the pre-trend result is "a failed parallel trends test and your entire causal interpretation is invalid," then says Figure 2 "clearly shows parallel trends visually" and that they are "uncertain whether to recommend rejection." So they make a categorical claim and then back away from it in the same comment.
2. **Appendix B vs. Figure 2.** The reviewer reads Appendix B as showing a significant deviation at t=-2 and Figure 2 as showing parallel trends. Whether those two readings actually conflict depends on your paper, which I can't see:
   - If Figure 2 plots the **same** pre-period coefficients as the Appendix B test, the t=-2 confidence interval should only just exclude zero. The reviewer may be misreading the plot, or the two may come from different specifications.
   - If Figure 2 comes from a **different** specification, sample or binning than the Appendix B test, the response has to explain the difference.
